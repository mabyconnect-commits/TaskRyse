About

Turning digital skill into opportunity across Africa

Taskryse connects people with legitimate AI, data and professional tasks from businesses and research organisations — and pays them in their local currency for work that passes review.

The name captures the journey: complete real tasks, grow verifiable skills and a strong contributor reputation, and rise to better opportunities. Nigeria-first, built to scale across the continent.
